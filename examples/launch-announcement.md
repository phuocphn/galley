# Introducing Meridian 2.0

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, teams are drowning in unstructured
data. That's why we're thrilled to announce Meridian 2.0, a game-changing
platform that empowers organizations to unlock the full potential of their
information architecture.

## What's new

Meridian 2.0 delivers a 40% improvement in query performance across the board.
Our completely redesigned indexing engine leverages cutting-edge techniques to
ensure your data is always at your fingertips, whenever you need it.

We've also introduced Collections, a powerful new way to organize related
documents. Collections can be shared across teams, nested to any depth, and
synced with your existing tools seamlessly.

## Getting started

Existing customers will be automatically upgraded over the coming weeks. New
users can start a free trial today and experience the difference for themselves.
