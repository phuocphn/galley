Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I migrate my data from another platform?
A: Absolutely! Meridian supports importing from most major platforms. Our
migration wizard will guide you through the process step by step, and most
migrations complete in under an hour.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Security is our top priority. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit
using industry-standard encryption, and we undergo regular third-party audits.

Q: What happens if I cancel?
A: You can cancel at any time. Your data remains accessible in read-only mode
for 30 days, after which it is permanently deleted.

Q: Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?
A: Yes. Reach out to our sales team to learn more about our nonprofit pricing.
